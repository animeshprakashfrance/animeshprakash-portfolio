01 — Origin to Present

A career built across four countries

01
Varanasi & Chennai, India
The foundation
Born in Varanasi, in northern India, in 1990; began his schooling there, then moved to Chennai, in southern India, for college — a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering at Sathyabama University, graduating 2012 — the foundation for everything that followed.
02
West Bengal & Odisha, India · 2012–2017
Blast furnace operations
Started as a Graduate Trainee at Rashmi Metaliks in West Bengal, moving through shift management, then to Visa Steel Limited in Odisha — five years of blast furnace operations and process control, capped by a stint in the Director's office gaining techno-commercial exposure: procurement, supplier negotiation, and cost analysis alongside the technical work.
03
Silvassa, India · 2017–2019
The pivot to stone wool
Joined Rockwool India as Production & Process Head — the move from iron & steel into mineral wool (building materials) manufacturing that would define the next decade.
04
Fujairah, UAE · 2019–2022
Process Engineer to Department Manager
Joined Fujairah Rockwool as Process Engineer across hot end and cold end, then rose to Department Manager — running a 180+ member plant team, holding the line on plant KPIs across efficiency, safety and output, and delivering energy and recycling initiatives worth over 1.7M AED in combined savings.
05
Bridgend, Wales, UK · 2022–2025
Process Engineer & Hot End Manager
Joined as Process Engineer across three UK manufacturing lines, promoted to Hot End Manager, then Process Lead — optimizing resin and melting materials for £1.6M in annual value.
06
France · 2025–Present
Senior Process Engineer, Saint-Gobain
Now on Saint-Gobain's central team — supporting factories and R&D worldwide, and leading greenfield and brownfield project work, from design validation through Factory Acceptance Tests (FATs) — the fourth country, and the widest reach yet.
