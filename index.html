01 Origin to present

A Career Built Across Four Countries

From blast furnaces in eastern India to the central process team of a global building-materials group in France — each move added scope: technical, then people, then multi-site, then multi-country.

1990 — 2012
01
Varanasi & Chennai, India

The Foundation

Born in Varanasi, in northern India. Schooling began there, before a move to Chennai, in southern India, for college — a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering at Sathyabama University, graduating in 2012 — the foundation for everything that followed.

2012 — 2017
02
West Bengal & Odisha, India

Blast Furnace Operations

Started as a Graduate Trainee at Rashmi Metaliks in West Bengal, moved through shift management, then on to Visa Steel Limited in Odisha — five years of blast furnace operations and process control, leading a team of 70+ across 250m³ and 215m³ furnaces, and capped by a stint in the Director’s office that added techno-commercial exposure: procurement, supplier negotiation and cost analysis alongside the technical work.

2017 — 2019
03
Silvassa, India

The Pivot to Stone Wool

Joined Rockwool India as Production & Process Head — the move from iron & steel into mineral wool (building materials) manufacturing that would define the next decade.

2019 — 2022
04
Fujairah, UAE

Process Engineer to Department Manager

Joined Fujairah Rockwool as Process Engineer across hot end and cold end, then rose to Department Manager with de facto plant-wide responsibility — holding the line on plant KPIs across a 180+ member team, efficiency, safety and output, and delivering energy and recycling initiatives worth over 1.7M AED in combined savings.

2022 — 2025
05
Bridgend, Wales, United Kingdom

Process Engineer, then Hot End Manager & Process Lead

Joined ROCKWOOL UK as Process Engineer across three manufacturing lines, then held Hot End Manager and Process Lead together — leading the hot end area, all CAPEX projects and process trials, and optimising resin utilisation and melting materials for £1.6M in annual value.

2025 — Present
06
Paris, France

Senior Process Engineer, Saint-Gobain

Now on Saint-Gobain’s central team — delivering process support to factories worldwide, supporting R&D, and leading greenfield and brownfield project work from design validation through Factory Acceptance Tests (FATs) — the fourth country, and the widest reach yet.
