Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information animeshprakash.com collects, why, and what choices you have. This is a personal professional website — it does not sell anything, and it does not collect more information than necessary to run the site and understand who visits it.

Information collected automatically

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic — for example, how many people visit, which pages are read, and roughly which country or device they use. This is aggregated, statistical information; it is not used to identify you personally.

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to support this measurement. You can decline this when the cookie banner appears, or at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site.

Information you provide

If you email or message me through a link on this site (for example via the "Get in touch" button or LinkedIn), I receive whatever you choose to send — typically your name, email address, and message. That information is used only to reply to you and is not shared with third parties or added to any marketing list.

Cookies

The only cookies this site uses are those set by Google Analytics, and only after you accept the cookie banner. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — traffic measurement (see Google's privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces on this page.
  • Netlify — hosts this website and processes standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) for security and reliability.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then automatically deleted. Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to respond and for ordinary record-keeping.

Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU, or elsewhere with data protection rights, you can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Since this site collects very little personal data in the first place, most requests can be resolved simply by contacting me directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — email contact@animeshprakash.com.
